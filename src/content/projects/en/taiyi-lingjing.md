## What it is

TaiYi Lingjing is a **proposed** AI-native scientific discovery environment: a **planned** loop that starts from a biological question and ends with a ranked, evidence-backed experiment plan.

It is **intended** to be organised around five subsystems — **Bio**, **Discovery**, **Experiment**, **Evidence**, **Human** — with agents **meant** to coordinate the hand-offs between them.

## Why it matters

The bottleneck in discovery is usually not generating hypotheses but judging which one is worth paying to test, and whether the evidence behind that judgement can be traced.

This is a research direction, not a delivered capability.
